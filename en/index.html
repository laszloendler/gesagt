The minutes write themselves on your iPhone.

GESAGT. records meetings, calls and dictation. Afterwards you see who said what, what was decided, and who does what by when. Everything runs on the device, with no account and no upload.

Download on the App Store

Free to download. GESAGT. Pro is a one-time purchase you can try free for 14 days. For iPhone with iOS 27.

Item 1

You record, the app takes the minutes.

  1. Tap Record and put your iPhone on the table. Ask everyone in the conversation first. GESAGT. reminds you.
  2. When the conversation ends, the app detects the language, English or German, separates the speakers and writes the note. Meetings get decisions, open points and to-dos with owner and due date. A call gets its purpose and agreements, a dictation gets your thoughts in order.
  3. Tap Done. The recording is deleted, the note stays.

If the app can't find the moment a to-do came from, it drops the to-do before it reaches your list.

Item 2

In the transcript, you're “Me”.

You read your voice in once during setup. You name everyone else after the recording, with a short audio sample to help. The name then appears wherever that person shows up, including their to-dos.

Me Mara Lindqvist Speaker 3 Play the sample, then name them
Item 3

The note lands in your Obsidian vault.

Pick your vault once. Every note becomes a Markdown file there, with frontmatter, to-dos in Tasks plugin format and the transcript in a collapsible callout.

Rename someone later and the app rewrites the same file. Anything you added in Obsidian yourself stays put.

Sharing as Markdown always works. Writing to the vault automatically is part of GESAGT. Pro.

Item 4

What's said in the room stays on your iPhone.

  • The app uploads nothing. A note only goes where you send it.
  • Audio is deleted as soon as you finish the note, after 24 hours at the latest.
  • The history covers 30 days, adjustable, and is excluded from iCloud backup.
  • Face ID can lock the app if you want.
  • The speech models are downloaded once from Apple by the system. After that everything works in airplane mode.
  • No account, no sign-in, no server listening in.
